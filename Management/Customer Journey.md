## Customer Journey (created by AI have to check it) 

### End User (In-Venue Fan)
1. Pre-Event (Before entering the venue)
- Fan purchases ticket for a Brisbane 2032 event
- Receives confirmation email with mention of "Play the Games" — teaser for in-venue rewards
- Optional: sees social media posts from friends who already played and won prizes
2. Arrival & Queuing
- Fan arrives at venue, joins queue
- Sees Play the Games branding on entry screens and seat backs
- Dead time begins — instinct is to reach for phone and scroll
3. The Trigger Moment
- QR code appears on stadium screen during dead time
- Fan scans with phone camera
- Auto-connects to venue Wi-Fi → platform loads in under 3 seconds
- No app download, no password, no friction
4. Onboarding Choice
- Guest Mode — skip registration, play instantly for small immediate prizes (F&B discounts, merch offers)
- Account Mode — quick sign-up to accumulate points across multiple sessions and events, unlocking larger prizes
5. Playing
- Fan chooses a 2–3 minute mini-game or quiz
- Examples: "Why did that athlete get disqualified?", live heat prediction, athlete comparison
- Branded sponsor moment appears (skippable pre-roll or integrated game branding)
- Leaderboard appears on stadium screen — fan sees their section ranking live
6. Winning
- Prize awarded instantly to phone (digital voucher, wallet pass)
- Location-aware offer: "Redeem your free drink at Stand C — 50 metres away"
- Fan walks to concession, redeems prize, returns to seat
7. Re-Engagement
- Play resumes — fan is back in the moment
- Now understands the sport better (rules learned through gameplay)
- Feels personally invested in the outcome
- Shares result on social media → organic word of mouth
8. Post-Event
- Account Mode fans receive summary of points earned
-  Notification of upcoming events where they can continue accumulating
-  Prize-driven incentive to attend another event
 
### Brand / Sponsor (The Real Customer)
1. Awareness
- Brand's sponsorship team already has a relationship with a venue (e.g. Queensland Stadiums)
- Venue introduces Play the Games as a new inventory option within their existing sponsorship portfolio
- Brand sees it as an extension of their current stadium presence — not a replacement
2. Consideration
- Play the Games presents the value proposition:
    - Active, measurable engagement vs. passive billboard impressions
    - Guaranteed captive audience during dead time
    - Direct link between brand activation and in-venue consumer behaviour
- Brand compares to existing sponsorship assets — sees clear differentiation
3. Decision & Deal
- Brand selects sponsorship package:
    - Branded game sessions (logo, colours, game theming)
    - Sponsored prize redemptions (e.g. "This free Coke is brought to you by X")
    - Pre-roll skippable ad within the game
- Revenue share agreed between venue and Play the Games
- Brand pays venue — venue shares agreed % with Play the Games
4. Activation
- Brand submits assets via sponsorship CMS (logo, ad creative, prize co-funding)
- Play the Games integrates branding into game sessions — seamless, non-intrusive
- Brand receives dashboard access: live view of sessions, engagement rates, redemptions
5. During the Event
- Brand's game goes live during dead-time windows
- Fans interact with branded content — not just see it
- Every redemption is tracked: brand knows exactly how many fans engaged, for how long, and what they redeemed
6. Post-Event Reporting
- Brand receives full performance report:
    - Number of sessions played
    -  Average time spent per session
    - Prize redemption rate
    - Concession uplift data
    - Audience demographic insights (opt-in only)
- Far more measurable than any billboard or LED board
7. Renewal & Upsell
- Strong performance data makes renewal an easy conversation
- Brand increases spend for next tournament / Olympic season
- Option to expand into more venues or exclusive category sponsorship
- Long-term: brand becomes official Play the Games partner across all Australian venues
