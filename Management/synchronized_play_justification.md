# Justifying Synchronized Play - Venue-Flexible Timing

## The Core Distinction

We don't dictate the exact minute the game launches - that's left to the venue, since they know their own crowd flow best. What we require is that within whatever moment they choose, everyone plays the same synchronized session together. The *when* is flexible. The *togetherness* is not.

## Why Synchronization Is the Real Design Choice

**Social Influence requires simultaneity, not just timing.** Per UTAUT, Social Influence - fans engaging because they see others around them engaging - only works if the engagement is visibly shared. A section leaderboard and a stadium screen showing live winners only create that effect if everyone plays at once. Spread across a flexible window, no one ever sees the full picture of who's winning or playing, and the shared spectacle disappears.

**Venues already know their own crowd flow - so let them decide.** Concourse width, concession layout, and crowd composition vary by venue. Rather than mandating one fixed trigger time everywhere, the platform lets each venue choose the optimal moment for their own audience. What stays constant is that the whole crowd plays together, whenever that moment is.

**This also strengthens the sponsor pitch.** Sponsors aren't being sold a rigid, identical schedule across every venue - they're guaranteed the one thing that matters commercially: a single, countable, stadium-wide moment of attention, every time, regardless of which minute it launches.

## The Rebuttal, In One Paragraph

We've had the question - why not let fans play whenever they want, so more people can participate? The answer is that the value isn't just that fans play, it's that they play *together*. That synchronized moment is what activates the social dynamics that make the format work, and what makes the inventory valuable to sponsors. What we have built in is flexibility on the venue side: each venue decides exactly when within their dead-time window to trigger the session, based on what they know about their own crowd. Venues get operational flexibility to optimise for their specific audience - but every fan in their seat experiences the same synchronized moment together.
