## The solution 
Play the Games is a sponsor-presented mini-game and quiz layer designed to beat the scroll. On the seat before the official game starting fans play instead of swipe. They earn sponsor-funded rewards, and they absorb the rules of the sport along the way. Use the phone to get people off their phones. 
Play the Games ‒ short, sport-themed mini-games and quizzes that activate the dead-time windows and challenge fans to engage rather than scroll.
Personalised merchandise discounts ‒ tailored offers of 10–20% based on what a fan engages with, on an opt-in basis.
The winner of the game would be presented on the screen
How It Works
A QR code appears on the stadium screen during dead time. Fans scan it with their phone camera — no app download required — and are instantly connected to the venue's Wi-Fi and redirected to the [name] platform.
From there, fans choose how they want to play:
Guest mode — jump straight in and play for an immediate, redeemable prize at the venue (food, drinks, merchandise)
Account mode — create a profile to accumulate points across multiple sessions and venues, perfect for fans attending several events across the Games
The experience is frictionless by design. Scanning takes three seconds. The platform loads instantly. No passwords, no app stores, no barriers — just a fan, their phone, and a reason to stay engaged

## Future
Prize-driven rewards ‒ discounted tickets and a grand-prize trip to the Games, offering high engagement value at low marginal cost.
