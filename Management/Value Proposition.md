## Sponsors & Brands (The Payer)
### Value Proposition: Active, measurable in-venue engagement that converts existing sponsorship spend into trackable interactions and real-time concession/merch sales — not passive billboard impressions.

#### Customer Jobs

- Justify sponsorship spend to leadership
- Reach captive fans during dead-time
- Differentiate from competing brands at the venue

#### Pains

- Paying for static assets with limited measurability
- Impressions, not interactions
- No clear data on what actually reaches fans

#### Gains

- Stronger renewal case with venues
- Provable ROI vs. static signage
- Access to new digital engagement category

#### Products & Services

- Branded mini-games and quizzes (logo, colours, theming)
- Pre-roll skippable in-game ads
- Sponsored prize redemption mechanic
- Engagement analytics dashboard

#### Pain Relievers

- Replaces unmeasurable static impressions with interaction data
- No new infrastructure or capital investment required
- Extends, doesn't cannibalise, existing venue relationships

#### Gain Creators

- Real performance data: sessions played, time spent, questions answered
- Branded game moments and sponsored prize redemptions
- Direct link between activation and in-venue spend
- New inventory category alongside existing sponsorship assets


## In-Venue Fans (The User)
### Value Proposition: Frictionless, free entertainment during dead-time that turns waiting into winning — instant prizes, sport knowledge, and a personal stake in the game, no app or account required.

#### Customer Jobs

- Fill dead-time during breaks/half-time
- Understand a sport they're unfamiliar with
- Get value (discounts, prizes) out of attending

#### Pains

- Attention defaults to platforms the venue doesn't control
- Boredom and disengagement during breaks in play
- Confusion watching sports they don't know the rules of

#### Gains

- Free rewards usable right where they're standing
- Feels more connected to the event, not just watching it
- Walks away knowing more than they arrived with

#### Products & Services

- QR-scan mini-games and quizzes, no download or account
- GPS-triggered in-venue food/drink offers
- 60-second rule explainers before unfamiliar sports
- Optional account mode for points and bigger prizes

#### Pain Relievers

- Removes the urge to default to social media scrolling
- No cost, no app, no friction to participate
- Demystifies sports the fan doesn't know well

#### Gain Creators

- Instant, immediately redeemable prizes (food, drink, merch)
- Learn-the-game tutorials build genuine sport understanding
- Personal stake in the live event in front of them
- Shareable results drive social bragging rights
