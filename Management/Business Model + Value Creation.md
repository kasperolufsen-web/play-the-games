## Does it make business sense to do it 

### Business Model (reference slides!)
Play the Games operates as a B2B2C revenue share model:
- We enter strategic partnerships with venues
- Venues already hold sponsorship relationships with major brands
- We extend their commercial inventory by creating a new, interactive advertising format, dead-time engagement slots
- Brands pay the venue for access to this format; Play the Games receives an agreed revenue share percentage of that incremental sponsorship income
- Revenue share split: to be negotiated per venue partnership
