# Showdown — Story Mode demo: run-of-show & narration

A frame-by-frame talking track for the 8-frame **Platform Walkthrough** (Story
Mode) — the end-user (fan) journey only. The operator/commercial dashboard is a
**separate** segment at the end, not part of the fan walkthrough. Advance one
frame, read the **SAY** line, advance again. Two cuts included: a **full ~1:50
version** and a **tight ~55s version** (use the short lines only).

**All money is AUD (say "Aussie dollars" or "A$" at least once).**

**Delivery notes**
- Pace ≈ 2 words per second. Don't rush frames 6, 7 and 9 — let the animation land before you talk over it.
- Speak to the *outcome* on each frame, not the label. Warm, confident, not hyped.
- One breath between frames while you click to the next dot.
- Recording tip: full-screen the app, hide the cursor, advance with the frame dots (bottom-right) or arrow keys. Do a silent run first to learn timing, then record narration in one take.

---

## Cold open — title / logo  (~5s)

**SAY:** "This is Showdown — live, in-stadium fan engagement. Here's everything that happens in the minutes before kickoff."

---

## Frame 1 · Dead Time Begins  (~12s)
*On screen: pre-kickoff wait, fans seated early, minutes to kickoff.*

**SAY (full):** "Every match has dead time — and the biggest chunk is before it even starts. Gates open, fans are in their seats, but kickoff is still minutes away. Fifty thousand people sit waiting, screens idle. Showdown activates that pre-kickoff window on purpose — so it never competes with the half-time food-and-drink rush."

**SAY (short):** "The biggest dead time is before kickoff — fans seated, waiting, screens idle. Showdown fills it, and leaves half-time F&B alone."

---

## Frame 2 · Stadium Screen Activates  (~10s)
*On screen: Showdown Arena, access code BRONCOS, suncorp.live, giant QR.*

**SAY (full):** "The venue operator flips it on. The hundred-foot Jumbotron becomes the Showdown Arena — a live entry code, a match portal, and one giant scannable QR."

**SAY (short):** "The operator activates it. The big screen becomes the arena — with one giant scannable QR."

---

## Frame 3 · Fan Scans QR  (~9s)
*On screen: phone camera recognition, "captured", under 2 seconds.*

**SAY (full):** "A fan lifts their phone. Their normal camera scans the screen from their seat and connects them in under two seconds — no app install, no typing."

**SAY (short):** "A fan scans with their normal camera — connected in under two seconds. No install."

---

## Frame 4 · Open App or Web Browser  (~11s)  — *the dual-client moment*
*On screen: Browser mode ("play in 3s · no install") vs Club App link ("saved wallet · push · loyalty").*

**SAY (full):** "One QR, two clients. If a fan has the club app it deep-links straight in — saved wallet, push and loyalty. If not, a lightweight web app boots in the browser and they're playing in about three seconds. Browser for the moment, app for the season."

**SAY (short):** "One QR, two clients — instant browser play, or the app for a saved wallet and loyalty."

---

## Frame 5 · Continue as Guest or Create Account  (~9s)
*On screen: handle "Renah Rocket", seat Block 207, Enter Arena.*

**SAY (full):** "Friction kills conversion, so guest mode is the default. Pick a nickname, choose your seat section, and you're in. Accounts are optional — for saving points and perks."

**SAY (short):** "Guest mode by default — a nickname and a seat, and you're playing. Accounts optional."

---

## Frame 6 · Crowd Connects  (~10s)  — *let the 0:03 countdown play*
*On screen: countdown, 14,108 phones connected, haptic rumble ready.*

**SAY (full):** "The countdown hits. Stadium servers sync sub-hundred-millisecond heartbeats to thousands of phones at once — and every device rumbles together to build the anticipation."

**SAY (short):** "Thousands of phones sync in real time — and rumble together as the countdown drops."

---

## Frame 7 · Mini-Game Starts  (~11s)  — *hold on the meter a beat*
*On screen: Power Strike, North Stand 84,200 vs South 79,900.*

**SAY (full):** "Showdown is live. Fans play Power Strike — a precision kick meter where the phone is the controller. The big screen tallies stand-versus-stand in real time, North Stand pulling ahead."

**SAY (short):** "Now they play. The phone is the controller; the screen tallies stand-versus-stand live."

---

## Frame 8 · Leaderboard and Reward  (~11s)  — *final fan frame; let the reward ticket reveal*
*On screen: North Stand wins, Renah Rocket 9,430 pts, free sponsor drink unlocked.*

**SAY (full):** "The stands battle for the top, and Block 207 takes it. Winning fans instantly unlock a sponsor-funded reward — a free drink, straight to their phone wallet, redeemable at the kiosk."

**SAY (short):** "A stand wins, and fans instantly unlock a sponsor-funded reward — straight to their phone."

---

## Close — the fan story  (~6s)
*On screen: hold on Frame 8, or cut back to logo.*

**SAY:** "Dead time, turned into live engagement. Browser for the moment, app for the season. That's Showdown — the stadium becomes the game."

## SEPARATE SEGMENT — the operator / commercial dashboard
*This is NOT part of the fan walkthrough. Switch deliberately: "That's the fan
side. Now the venue's side." It's a different audience — the operator, not the

**SAY:** "For the venue, it's an operator console. Live connected fans, a 46% join rate, and 41 minutes of engagement in what used to be dead time."

*On screen: concession +A$48,200, sponsor activation package A$42,500, first-party data A$9,400, total +A$100,100.*

**SAY (full):** "And the commercial model: about A$48,000 in extra concessions, a A$42,500 sponsor activation package, and A$9,000 of first-party fan data — roughly A$100,000 of new value per match. Conservatively, without a premium sponsor package, still around A$65,000. Every figure's a modelled estimate, confirmed in a pilot."

**SAY (short):** "The commercial model: extra concessions, a sold sponsor package and fan data — around A$100,000 of new value per match, conservatively A$65,000. All modelled estimates."
